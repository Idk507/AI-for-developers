Scenario: An AI agent creates duplicate customer records
Imagine you're responsible for an enterprise AI assistant that can create support tickets through external tools.

A customer reports that the assistant created three duplicate tickets after a temporary service outage.

Your engineering team discovers that the retry mechanism executed the same operation multiple times.

Interview question: How would you explain the incident to the customer, contain the immediate problem, and coordinate a reliable long-term fix?

Prepare a two-minute response using the Situation, Task, Action, and Result framework.

Focus on how you would communicate the customer impact, distinguish confirmed findings from assumptions, mitigate further duplicate creation, and verify that the fix works.

Explain how you would collaborate with backend engineers, the support team, and the external service provider.
